# Dateline Weather — Support

Dateline Weather shows the current conditions and today's temperatures next to the date on your iPhone Lock Screen.

## Setting up the widget

1. Open the app and allow location access, or choose a place under **Settings > Location**.
2. Start your free week from the app.
3. Touch and hold your Lock Screen, tap **Customize**, then choose the Lock Screen.
4. Tap the date above the clock, then choose **Dateline** and the style you want.
5. Tap **Done**.

The Styles tab in the app shows what each style looks like. To switch styles later, repeat steps 3 to 5. The slot beside the date holds one widget, so Dateline replaces whatever is there now. The app shows the same steps under **Settings > How to Add the Widget**.

## Settings

Open the app and tap **Settings**.

- **Location.** The app and the widget show the weather where you are. To show another place, search for a city or address and tap the result. While a place is chosen, the app and the widget stop using your location. Tap **Current Location** to go back.
- **Temperature.** Automatic follows your region. You can also choose Fahrenheit or Celsius.
- **Tap Opens.** Choose whether tapping the widget opens Apple Weather or Dateline. iOS opens Dateline first and hands off to Apple Weather right away.
- **Show a Countdown.** When rain or snow is about to start, the widget counts down to it in place of the weather, for example "Rain within 15 min". **Style** changes how the countdown reads. The countdown depends on Apple's minute-by-minute forecast, which isn't available everywhere.

## Subscription

The Lock Screen widget needs a subscription. The app itself, including today's weather, is free.

- **Free trial.** New subscribers get one week free. Cancel at least 24 hours before it ends and you won't be charged.
- **Canceling.** In the app, open **Settings** and tap **Manage**. You can also cancel in the iOS Settings app under **your name > Subscriptions**.
- **New phone or reinstall.** In the app, open **Settings** and tap **Restore Purchases**, signed in with the same Apple Account you subscribed with.

## Common issues

- **The widget says "Start free trial" or "Tap to subscribe".** The widget needs an active subscription. Tap it to open the app. If you already subscribed, tap **Restore Purchases** in Settings.
- **The widget says "Open Dateline".** The app hasn't asked for your location yet. Open it once and allow location access.
- **The widget says "location off".** Location permission was denied or removed. Turn it on in the iOS Settings app under **Privacy & Security > Location Services > Dateline**, set to "While Using the App or Widgets". You can also choose a place under **Settings > Location** in the app, which needs no location permission.
- **The widget says "no location", "no forecast" or "weather failed".** The phone couldn't get a position or Apple's weather service didn't answer. The widget tries again in about 15 minutes. Opening the app and pulling down on the Today tab fetches the weather right away.
- **The widget shows old weather.** The widget asks for new weather about every 30 minutes during the day, every hour between midnight and 6 a.m., and more often when rain or snow is close. iOS decides the exact moment and can delay it. Opening the app refreshes the widget.
- **The countdown never appears.** Check that **Show a Countdown** is on in Settings. Apple's minute-by-minute forecast covers only some regions, and the widget can't count down without it.
- **A TestFlight build stopped launching.** Beta builds expire after 90 days. Install the latest build from the TestFlight app.
