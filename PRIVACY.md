# Dateline Weather — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** July 10, 2026

Dateline Weather is an iOS app that shows the current weather conditions and temperatures next to the date on your Lock Screen.

## What the app does with your data

- **Location.** With your permission, the app reads your device's location on-device and sends the coordinates to **Apple's WeatherKit service** to retrieve the weather for where you are. That request goes to Apple, not to us. Apple's handling of it is governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) and the [Apple Weather data sources page](https://developer.apple.com/weatherkit/data-source-attribution/).
- **Nothing is collected by the developer.** We operate no servers. The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs. Your location and weather data are cached only on your device, and only so the widget can show the last known weather when the network is unavailable.
- **Beta builds.** If you use the app through TestFlight, Apple may collect crash logs and usage statistics and share them with us as described in the [TestFlight terms](https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/testflight/).

## Your controls

You can revoke location access at any time in **Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services**. The app will fall back to its last cached weather and then stop displaying data.
