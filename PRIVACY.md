# Dateline Weather — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 25, 2026

Dateline Weather is an iOS app that shows the current weather conditions and temperatures next to the date on your Lock Screen.

## What the app does with your data

- **Location.** With your permission, the app reads your device's location on-device and sends the coordinates to **Apple's WeatherKit service** to retrieve the weather for where you are. The app also sends the coordinates to Apple's geocoding service to show the name of your town or city. Both requests go to Apple, not to us. Apple's handling of them is governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) and the [Apple Weather data sources page](https://developer.apple.com/weatherkit/data-source-attribution/).
- **Purchases.** Subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple. We never receive your payment details, your name, or your Apple Account. The app asks Apple's StoreKit service whether your subscription is active and keeps that answer on your device so the widget can check it.
- **Nothing is collected by the developer.** We operate no servers. The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs. Your location, the latest weather, your subscription status, and your settings (such as °F or °C) are stored only on your device, in storage shared between the app and its widget.
- **Beta builds.** If you use the app through TestFlight, Apple may collect crash logs and usage statistics and share them with us as described in the [TestFlight terms](https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/testflight/).

## Your controls

You can revoke location access at any time in **Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services**. The app will fall back to its last cached weather and then stop displaying data. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your device.
