# Dateline Weather — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 12, 2026

Dateline Weather is an iOS app that shows the current weather conditions and temperatures next to the date on your Lock Screen.

## What the app does with your data

- **Location.** With your permission, the app and its widget read your device's location on-device and send the coordinates to **Apple's WeatherKit service** to retrieve the weather for where you are. The app also sends the coordinates to Apple's geocoding service to show the name of your town or city. Both requests go to Apple, not to us. Apple's handling of them is governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) and the [Apple Weather data sources page](https://developer.apple.com/weatherkit/data-source-attribution/).
- **A place you choose.** Under **Settings > Location** you can search for a city or address and use it in place of your current location. The text you type is sent to Apple's geocoding service to find matching places. While a place is chosen, the app and the widget send that place's coordinates to WeatherKit and do not read your device's location.
- **Purchases.** Subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple. We never receive your payment details, your name, or your Apple Account. The app asks Apple's StoreKit service whether your subscription is active and keeps that answer on your device so the widget can check it.
- **Nothing is collected by the developer.** We operate no servers. The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs. It sends no notifications. Your location, a place you chose, the latest weather, your subscription status, and your settings (such as °F or °C) are stored only on your device, in storage shared between the app and its widget.
- **Beta builds.** If you use the app through TestFlight, Apple may collect crash logs and usage statistics and share them with us as described in the [TestFlight terms](https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/testflight/).

## Your controls

You can revoke location access at any time in **Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services**. The app will fall back to its last cached weather for up to three hours and then stop displaying data, unless you have chosen a place under **Settings > Location** in the app. To stop using a chosen place, select **Current Location** there. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your device.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published in the app and wherever the policy is posted online, with a new effective date.
